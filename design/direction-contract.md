# Surface brief: the whole app (development only, never shipped)

Scope: every screen of Dil Se Hindi. Mode: Operate, with Experience moments (results, stories, love notes).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Your vocabulary is a rangoli you are making for her. Every phrase you learn lays down a dot of gulal pigment, and each screen is coloured by the stage you are in. It refuses the category's cartoon learning path and grey flashcards, and this app's own previous grouped-list kit.

OWN-WORLD: Gulal, the powder of Holi. Night ground of deep indigo (#15102E) or day ground of moonstone white (#F7F5FB). Five pigments, one per stage: rani pink, kesari saffron, mor peacock, neel indigo, haldi turmeric; hara green and laal red only for right and wrong. Each top-level screen opens with one field drenched in powder that owns the top region; below it, quiet native rows on the ground. Progress is drawn in dots, never bars or rings. Eczar carries display type in both scripts; the system face carries everything else.

STORY: He opens the app, sees tonight's rangoli with his newest word at its heart, taps the one obvious action (review what's due, or continue the next lesson), works through a deck of pigment cards, and returns to watch the new dots bloom into the rangoli.

FIRST VIEWPORT (Today): greeting in Hindi set large in Eczar with a one-line English status; a streak diya at the right. A powder field fills the next 60% of the screen with the rangoli (newest word in the centre medallion, tap to hear it), the count sentence, and the primary action as a full-width button at the field's foot. Below the fold edge: the next lesson as a small deck of stacked cards. Floating glass tab bar: Today, Journey, Practice, Words, Me.

FORM: iOS app shell (tab bar, large titles collapsing to an inline bar, sheets, grouped lists) with the world lending palette, display type, density and the rangoli. Position 1 of 4 directions offered; the user locked it. seed: none (unavailable).

SIGNATURE: Rangoli bloom. When he returns from a lesson, the dots he just earned are thrown in from the field's edge like powder and settle into the pattern, then the centre word changes. Motion grammar: deck cards slide away and the next lifts from the stack; correct answers throw a small burst of pigment; sheets rise; the tab lens slides. Exponential ease-out, 0.3 to 0.6 s, crossfades only under Reduce Motion.
