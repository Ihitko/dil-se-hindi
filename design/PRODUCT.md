# Dil Se Hindi

## What it is
A personal Hindi course for someone learning the language to talk with his girlfriend and her family. It runs as a claude.ai artifact and as an installable iPhone home-screen app built from the same file.

## Who uses it, and where
One learner, on an iPhone, in short sessions: evenings in a dim room as often as daytime breaks. The phone follows the system light/dark setting, so both appearances are first-class. He is motivated by love, not grades, and wants the app itself to feel premium, colourful and Apple-native.

## What it must do
- Teach speaking Hindi in 47 units across 5 stages (513 phrases), plus 8 units of Devanagari reading.
- Schedule every learned item with spaced repetition and make "review what's due" the easiest action in the app.
- Offer practice beyond lessons: 17 graded stories, 11 conversation scenes, love notes to send her, verb/number/time gyms, a phrasebook, and live conversation with Claude where the runtime allows it.
- Keep progress safe: synced to the Claude account inside claude.ai, stored locally in the home-screen app, with a progress code to move between them.

## Product truths that do not change
- All Hindi content, grammar notes, drills, scoring, spaced-repetition rules and the answer checker are reviewed and stay as they are.
- Gender-aware Hindi (the learner can be a man or a woman), romanization that fades as words get strong, audio with a slow mode.
- Respect for elders' register (aap, ji) is part of what is taught.

## Mode
Operate: a daily-use app. Platform conventions win for navigation and controls (tab bar for sections, large titles, sheets for focused tasks, grouped lists for settings, 44 pt targets, system text for body copy). The brand speaks through palette, display type, density and one signature move.

## Anti-references
- The previous look of this app: iOS grouped lists of identical rows and cards, gradient-wash hero cards, Fitness-style rings, eyebrow labels, gradient text.
- Category defaults: Duolingo's cartoon path and green buttons; Anki's grey flashcards; claymorphism and indigo "edtech" palettes.

## Success
Opening the app should make him want to keep going: what to do next is obvious within a second, the screen looks like nothing else on his phone, and progress feels like something he is making for her.
