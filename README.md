# Dil Se Hindi

Learn Hindi for the one you love: 55 units in 5 stages plus Devanagari reading, stories, conversation scenes, verb, number and time gyms, love notes, and spaced repetition. Your vocabulary grows into a rangoli, one dot of colour per phrase.

**Install on iPhone:** open https://ihitko.github.io/dil-se-hindi/ in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Works offline, in light and dark mode. Progress is stored on your device; use Me → Move your progress to copy it between devices.
